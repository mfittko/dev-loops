### Fixed

- **`pre_approval_gate` fails closed when Copilot is enabled but no round was driven for the current head (issue [#2146](https://github.com/mfittko/dev-loops/issues/2146)).** The independent gate-ENTRY re-check in `evaluatePrGateCoordination` (`applyUnsettledCopilotReviewEntryGuard`) previously refused pre-approval/final-approval entry only while a Copilot review request was still *outstanding* (`requested`/`already-requested`, #1190). It now also fails closed on the absent / never-driven case: when `maxCopilotRounds > 0` (Copilot enabled, not `internal_only`), the request status is non-outstanding, there is no clean same-head submitted review (`sameHeadCleanConverged` false), and no Copilot review exists on the current head (`copilotReviewOnCurrentHead` false), it routes to `request_copilot_review` and forbids `run_pre_approval_gate`. The exemption is keyed on **current-head** evidence, never a raw across-PR `copilotReviewRoundCount` — a PR with rounds on a prior head and a new current head that carries no review (interpreter state `low_signal_converged` with `copilotReviewOnCurrentHead: false`) still fails closed. This closes the bypass where a grant-y lifecycle label (a stale/racy `low_signal_converged`, or one carried by prior-head rounds) let an agent reach a clean pre-approval verdict without the loop ever driving a Copilot round on the head under review — convergence must be a round the loop drove and awaited, not GitHub's incidental auto-review nor a stale prior-head round. A clean same-head submitted Copilot review (including GitHub's auto-review) still satisfies convergence with no redundant re-request; the post-cap round-cap-clean fallback, `maxCopilotRounds: 0`, and `internal_only` PRs are unchanged.
