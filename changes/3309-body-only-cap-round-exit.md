### Fixed

- A judge decision with `remedyKind: "pr_body"` marks a finding whose only remedy is a PR-body edit. `isDeltaBodyItem` routes it to every body-only exit (`delta_body_only_act_set`, the `loop gate-coordination` body-only branch, `composeBodyOnlyConvergedDraftGate`) even when its scope names a file, so a cap round whose act items are all PR-body items exits by the body-only converged post instead of a refused fixer or chain-extension route. `spec-authority`, `judge-pass` and `write-gate-findings-log` accept the value and carry it into the ledger (#3309)
- `write-gate-findings-log.mjs --rejudge-same-round` rewrites the live same-head ledger in place, keeping its round number and archiving nothing, so a cap-round ledger can gain `remedyKind` without becoming round N+1. The write is an overlay: only `remedyKind: "pr_body"` is added to act findings, matched by fingerprint, and every other field of the live ledger is kept. It refuses with `rejudge-no-live-ledger`, `rejudge-fingerprint-mismatch`, `rejudge-field-changed`, `rejudge-round-incomplete` or `rejudge-gate-unsupported` (`GATE-LEDGER-REJUDGE-SAME-ROUND`) (#3309)
- A review-thread act item with no file now refuses `body_item_locatable` with a thread-specific message, and a `remedyKind` outside `evidence_only` and `pr_body` is refused by the ledger writer, the spec-authority validator and the comment mappers (#3309).
