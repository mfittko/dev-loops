### Added

- **Holistic reviewer angle: an independent, un-briefed, general-purpose reviewer in the default gate fan-out (issue [#2307](https://github.com/mfittko/dev-loops/issues/2307)).** Both `draft_gate` and `pre_approval_gate` now dispatch a mandatory `holistic` angle: a senior-engineer, fresh-context reviewer that receives only the spec (acceptance criteria, definition of done, non-goals) and the diff — never a developer brief or steering from the reviewed party, unlike the developer-briefed Pre-PR reviewer (#2305). Its mandate is broad rather than a single named lens: it reads the whole change end to end and flags CROSS-CUTTING problems no narrow angle owns (mismatches between parts of the change, gaps between the diff and the acceptance criteria, unintended interactions across modules, missing pieces the spec implies). It dispatches as its own dedicated fan-out unit under the shipped `gates.fanout.groups` table (a singleton `holistic` group), so under the default config it is never co-batched with a narrow-lens angle; a consumer repo that overrides `gates.fanout.groups` must restate the `holistic` singleton to keep that guarantee, and its findings flow through the same fan-in + judge pipeline as every other angle.
