### Changed

- **Dev-loop coordinator contract: extended the blocking-wait mandate to CI-green and Copilot-review waits, and stated orchestrator-owned and fresh-context delegation invariants (issue [#2176](https://github.com/mfittko/dev-loops/issues/2176)).** The blocking-wait invariant in the `dev-loop` agent contract now covers every wait a run depends on — a judge, a fixer, a reviewer, a CI-green wait, or a Copilot-review wait — routing subagent-child waits to a blocking dispatch / `bg_wait` subscription and CI/Copilot waits to a bounded foreground probe, and explicitly forbidding a detached background wait that ends the turn. The Operating contract now states, uniformly, that merge, retrospective, and issue creation are orchestrator-owned (the child emits findings and stops at the human-approval checkpoint), and the Subagent delegation section states that every sub-loop runs in its own dedicated fresh-context agent. To keep the contract self-consistent, `issue-create` moved out of the child-sanctioned `edits` map into `orchestratorOwned` in `scripts/loop/sanctioned-commands.mjs`; the runtime child-origin enforcement check remains tracked by issue [#2173](https://github.com/mfittko/dev-loops/issues/2173).
