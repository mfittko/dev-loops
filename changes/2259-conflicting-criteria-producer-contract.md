- **The judge output contract now pins the `conflictingCriteria` field-shape the gate enforces, and the missing-field error names its rule (issue [#2259](https://github.com/mfittko/dev-loops/issues/2259)).** The judge persona (`agents/judge.agent.md`) now states that both conflict outcomes (`finding_conflicts`, `remediation_conflicts`) MUST carry a non-empty `conflictingCriteria` array of criterion ids — naming them in `rationale` prose alone does not satisfy it — matching what `SPEC-AUTHORITY-CONFLICT-EVIDENCE` enforces. An absent or empty `conflictingCriteria` on a conflict outcome now fails closed on that named rule instead of the generic `must be an array of criterion ids` message, so a human is routed to populate the field rather than hand-edit the verdict. The two conflict outcomes are exported once as `SPEC_AUTHORITY_CONFLICT_OUTCOMES`, and a divergence-guard test binds the enforcer and the documented contract to the same field and shape so producer and enforcer cannot silently drift apart.
