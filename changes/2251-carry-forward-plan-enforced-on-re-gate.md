### Fixed

- **Head-bump re-gates now fail closed unless the carry-forward resolver ran (issue [#2251](https://github.com/mfittko/dev-loops/issues/2251)).** `resolve-angle-carry-forward.mjs` records its plan as a keyed `<gate>-<headSha>.carry-forward-plan.json` artifact at the current head (on both the success and the fail-closed full-fallback paths), and `emit-fanout-dispatch.mjs` refuses to emit — spawning zero reviewers — on a re-gate head (a prior findings-log for the gate exists at an earlier head) when that artifact is absent (`GATE-EXEC-CARRY-FORWARD-PLAN-REQUIRED`). This closes the gap where a re-gate skipped carry-forward entirely and re-fanned every angle, rationalized by a non-existent "prior round not clean → nothing carries" rule. Carry-forward eligibility is unchanged: it is decided per angle by the delta against that angle's review surface (`clean` OR `findings_present`), never by the prior round's overall verdict. Only `draft_gate` / `pre_approval_gate` guard here; the review gate has no resolver.
