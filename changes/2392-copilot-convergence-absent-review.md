### Fixed

- **`copilot_convergence` no longer passes because no current-head Copilot review exists (issue [#2392](https://github.com/mfittko/dev-loops/issues/2392)).** `evaluateCopilotConvergence` now distinguishes three states (`current_head_clean`, `current_head_findings`, `no_current_head_review`) and reports them as `state`. A head without a current-head Copilot review converges only through a sanctioned disposition recorded for that head: `copilot_gate_disabled` (`maxCopilotRounds: 0`), `round_cap_clean_fallback` (round cap reached), or `docs_only_suppression` (the last reviewed head was clean and the delta since then is outside Copilot's review surface). Otherwise `merge-pr.mjs` refuses via `copilot_convergence`. The merge result and refusal now record `copilotConvergenceState` and the disposition that satisfied it, so `copilotDisposition: null` no longer appears on a merged PR. Clean, 🔵, 🟡, and unrecognized current-head reviews keep their existing decisions, and the round-cap gate-entry grant is unchanged.
