# 0041. Require the deslop and designer/vision review loops as fail-closed gates

## Status

Accepted — 2026-07-19 ([issue 1438](https://github.com/mfittko/dev-loops/issues/1438))

## Context

Two quality loops we rely on for prose and UI deliverables failed open. Prose had no deslop enforcement at all: the gate angle catalog (`packages/core/src/analysis/change-classifier.mjs`) maps `DOCS_ONLY` to `docs, link-check, contract-surface, dry`, with no deslop/contrast angle, and the A/B-contrast deslop step ([`ab-contrast-deslop-step.md`](../../skills/docs/ab-contrast-deslop-step.md)) was advisory prose that nothing triggered or blocked. For UI, only the auto-scoped e2e **smoke** gate (`article-smoke`/`deck-smoke`, path-triggered on `docs/articles/*.html` and `docs/presentations/*.html`) was required — it verifies fit and layout, not editorial or design quality — while the designer/vision review loop ([`ui-designer-review-loop.md`](../../skills/docs/ui-designer-review-loop.md)) was explicitly optional. The gap surfaced while scoping the evolution article/deck ([issue 1437](https://github.com/mfittko/dev-loops/issues/1437)), where the loops had to be hand-required through per-issue acceptance criteria — the band-aid pattern the repo otherwise fixes at source. This decision was escalated as an RFC and settled through a grill on [issue 1438](https://github.com/mfittko/dev-loops/issues/1438).

## Decision

We promote both loops from advisory/optional to required and fail-closed, auto-scoped by path, the way UI e2e smoke already is. The prose deslop loop becomes a required gate pass triggered by prose paths — `docs/articles/**`, `docs/presentations/**`, `README*`, and narrative `docs/*.md` — while normative contracts under `skills/docs/**` are exempt, because they are governed by the contract style guide and contradiction lens and deslop's contrast-cutting must not fight required RFC-2119 modality. The designer/vision review becomes required on the rendered-HTML paths that already gate smoke, enforced as a recorded-evidence pass modeled on gate-evidence: the loop runs, writes its artifact bundle plus a verdict carrying the visual bar, a cleared flag, and `axe` findings, and the gate blocks unless that evidence is present and cleared — autonomous, with no human required by default. Both requirements apply to all triggering diffs while honoring the existing light-mode and spike-mode relaxed-gate carve-outs. We rejected promoting only one loop (the originating need was both), a vision-model rubric score for the UI pass (too dependent on rubric quality to fail closed cleanly), a human-designer checkpoint (strongest but non-autonomous), and a size/impact threshold (adds a bypass vector and a knob to tune).

## Consequences

Prose and UI deliverables can no longer reach merge without a deslop pass and a designer/vision review; the guarantee is uniform and path-scoped rather than reconstructed per issue. The recorded-evidence shape keeps the UI requirement autonomous and consistent with how gate-evidence already fails closed, and the light/spike carve-outs keep small and exploratory work cheap. Enforcement is implementation work scheduled separately from this record: the two mechanisms are independent surfaces — a fan-out review angle for deslop in the angle resolver, and a recorded-evidence required check for designer review in the UI-scoping and gate-evidence path — and are expected to land as separate tracked issues referencing this ADR. That scheduling relies on the single-issue allowance for simple implementations rather than a mandatory epic tree.
